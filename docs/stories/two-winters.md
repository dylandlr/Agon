# Two Winters

*The band of seed 21, from their first spring to their last. Every event in this account is in the record (`events.jsonl`), in the order it happened; where the record gives a cause, the cause is given; where it does not, none is supplied. The names are for reading. Ash, Esk, Cael and Gant were women; Bren, Dov, Fenn and Hael were men.*

---

## The first spring

There were eight of them. They began on a strip of grass between the forest and the water, with tundra to the north. In the first half hour Bren, Cael and Dov dug roots in the same patch. Gant touched Esk, and touched her again each half hour through the night.

At half past one Cael aimed a push kick at Bren and missed. At two she took Ash's foot in a hold. The record gives no cause for either; it records no blow before them. By three all but Bren were asleep; Bren dug roots until half past three and lay down.

In the first week the band settled where the forest met the marsh. The marsh was drinkable from under the trees. Over two years, more than a thousand of their drinks were taken from two cells of that forest edge, and the band did not camp anywhere else. There were hives in the trees: Cael took honey on the second morning and was stung; Fenn on the third afternoon, and was stung. Cael would be stung robbing hives eight times in two years, Esk and Fenn twice each.

On the fourth evening Ash and Esk were both parched. Neither was parched again until Fenn, a year and a half later. On the sixth day Gant fell sick; the record names the water she drank. On the seventh Dov fell sick from the same cause. Each was well again within two days.

Bren and Cael lay together on the second morning; Esk and Fenn on the fourth, sixth and ninth days; Bren and Esk twice on the tenth. Gant was not recorded with anyone that spring.

On the tenth morning an aurochs that had been eating food Hael left on the ground was recorded at ease with him. The record calls it taming and names the cause: the food he left.

## The first summer

Cael was stung on the first night. On the third day she kicked Esk in the neck and was stung that afternoon; she was stung again before dawn on the fourth. On the fourth evening a crocodile caught Dov at the water's edge and took a quarter of his health; he was downed by the wound and stood again two and a half hours later.

On the fifth morning Cael took Bren's foot in a hold and he broke it. On the sixth, in the small hours, she kicked Fenn. On the eighth evening she butted Bren and moved him back; that night she was stung a fifth time. A heat wave began on the seventh day.

Bren and Esk lay together six times that summer. Ash lay with Fenn once and with Bren once. Esk and Fenn once.

On the ninth morning Dov took food Hael had left on the ground. On the tenth Hael took food Dov had left, twice. The record names the giver each time.

## Autumn

Ash fell sick from the water on the first afternoon. A cold snap set in that evening. By one in the morning Fenn was sick; the record names close contact with Ash. Cael was sick from the water on the fourth morning and Gant from contact with Cael by noon. Each recovered within three days.

The blows in autumn were more often answered. Fenn body-checked Bren in the night of the fifth day and clinched him that morning; at half past one Bren clinched Fenn, and the record marks it as striking back; Fenn broke it. Ash clinched, butted and kicked at Fenn over the fourth to seventh days; on the seventh morning Fenn tried a chokehold on Ash, marked as striking back, and she broke it. The weather cleared the same hour. On the ninth day the record notes drought.

Esk was stung on the third and sixth days. Bren lay with Gant on the second night and with Ash on the third; Bren and Esk were not recorded together again after summer. On the tenth day Hael fell sick from the water, and Dov from the water and from contact with Hael.

## The first winter

Fenn clinched Cael and grabbed Bren's leg on the first day. Hael and Dov recovered on the second. On the third morning the crocodile caught Dov again, a quarter of his health; he was down four hours. That afternoon Hael fell sick from the water. That night Bren struck at an animal; the record does not say which.

Cael and Fenn lay together on the second and fourth days; Cael and Bren on the fifth and ninth.

A cold snap set in at three in the afternoon on the fourth day. From the thermal constants of this world: under the snap the air in the forest fell to freezing and below, and at that temperature a body under the trees lost heat unless it was resting with at least two others beside it.

A wolf bit Ash at four in the morning on the fifth day. At eight that evening Esk was recorded freezing in air at minus nine. At half past ten Hael was freezing on the tundra edge at minus twenty-one. At three the next morning Ash was freezing at minus fifteen. At six Hael was downed; the record gives no cause.

Hael's record for the following day and a half, at the north water in the forest: on a bed of leaves; rising to drink at nearly every half hour, sixty-seven drinks in his last six days; roots gathered three times; four meals; three touches over the same six days, against the others' dozens. At three in the morning on the seventh day he was freezing in air at zero. He drank five times before dawn, slept an hour, dug roots at seven, drank at half past seven, half past eight, nine, and half past ten. At half past ten he died of cold, three days into the snap.

Esk's record from the fifth day: at the marsh camp; forty-six drinks and one gathering in six days. Starving by nine on the sixth evening. Downed at two in the morning on the eighth day. Dead of starvation at half past eight.

Dov was freezing on the seventh afternoon and again at midnight, and was downed on the eighth morning. Ash was downed at noon. Fenn was freezing in the small hours of the seventh at minus fourteen. At half past two on the eighth the record marks Dov as haunted, cause: nearly dying. Dov was down, with intervals, until the fifth morning of spring, one hundred and sixty-four hours after his last fall.

Ash died of cold at dawn on the ninth day, five days into the snap. That afternoon the record marks Fenn as hardened, cause: long hardship survived.

Three of eight died in the snap. Of the three, one was recorded alone, one had stopped eating, one had been moving between ten cells. None of the five who died later that year or the next died of cold.

## The second spring

Five remained: Bren, Cael, Dov, Fenn, Gant. Dov stood on the fifth day.

Cael held off a predator in the small hours of the second day, the first such record, and three hours later tried a chokehold on Fenn, which he broke. Drought began that afternoon. Bren fell sick from the water on the third night; Fenn from contact with Bren on the fourth; Gant from the water and from Bren on the fifth; Cael from Bren and Fenn that evening. All recovered within four days. Cael put Bren in a foot hold on the fourth day and kicked at him on the fifth; Fenn clinched Bren on the sixth. Fenn held off a predator on the eighth morning. Bren struck at an animal on the ninth. The drought continued.

Bren and Cael lay together on the fifth day. Of the twenty-eight pairings in the two-year record, Bren is in twenty.

## The second summer

Bren was starving on the first day. The weather cleared on the second; at two that afternoon Cael conceived. The record names Bren as the father. It is the only conception in the record.

Cael held off a predator on the third evening. On the eighth day: a wolf bit Dov at one in the morning; Bren fell sick at five, no cause given; the crocodile caught Bren at three in the afternoon, a quarter of his health, and he was downed; at half past four, down, he held it off; at seven he stood. He was downed again on the ninth morning, with no cause given. Fenn fell sick from the water and from Bren. At half past one that afternoon the record marks Bren as haunted. He recovered from the sickness on the tenth.

Bren and Gant lay together on the fifth and eighth days; Fenn and Gant on the eighth.

## The second autumn

A wolf bit Dov at half past four on the first morning; he was downed by the wound and stood in half an hour. Gant fell sick from the water. On the third morning Dov fell sick, cause not given, then fell, was downed by the wound, and died of the sickness at half past one. Bren held off a predator that night.

On the fifth afternoon the crocodile caught Bren; the recorded loss was two per cent of his health, and he died of predation at half past two. That evening Esk's body was recorded as bones. Gant took food Bren had left on the ground.

Drought on the seventh. Hael's body was bones on the eighth. On the ninth, Fenn was parched at two in the morning; Ash's body was bones at noon; Fenn was downed that night and died of thirst at dawn on the tenth. The record places him at the marsh camp.

## The second winter, and after

Two remained: Cael, pregnant, and Gant. A cold snap set in on the second morning; half an hour later the crocodile caught Cael, a quarter of her health; she was downed and stood three hours later. Gant was freezing at three in the morning on the fifth, sixth and seventh days, in air a little below zero, and was downed on the seventh. The weather cleared on the eighth.

On the ninth day Cael was stung taking honey, fell sick, cause not given, and was stung again an hour and a half later: her seventh and eighth stings. A second snap set in on the tenth; Gant fell sick from the water and from contact with Cael.

At nine on the first morning of the third spring the crocodile caught Cael at the water, a quarter of her health, and she died of predation. The record still has her pregnant. At three that afternoon Gant died of the sickness. The band had lasted two years and five days.

---

## What the record supports

- The camp was forest beside marsh. All drinking was done there. Twelve of nineteen infections name the water; six of six crocodile attacks were on camp cells; two deaths of disease and two of predation. The band was not recorded at any other water in two years. There are eighty-two water cells on the map.
- The cold snap killed three. Hael's six days before death show sixty-seven drinks, four meals and three touches; Esk's, forty-six drinks and one gathering; Ash's, movement across ten cells and a wolf bite. The five who slept together through the snap lived.
- Fenn died of thirst at the camp, during a drought, beside the marsh.
- Drinks per day over each life: Hael 12, Dov 10, Gant 7, Cael 6.5, Esk 6, Fenn 5.6, Ash 5.3, Bren 5.2. Meals per day: 2.5 to 7.6.
- Twenty-eight pairings, one conception. Two of four women died in the first winter. Bren, in twenty of the pairings, was bitten, sick, downed and marked haunted through the second summer and died in the second autumn.
- Thirty-two blows between band members, none fatal; two marked as striking back; no cause recorded for the rest.
- Honey was taken eleven times; the takers were stung twelve times.
- Food left on the ground was taken by another five times. Predators were held off five times. One animal was recorded tame. Bedding was used seven hundred and twenty-two times. No fire was recorded.
