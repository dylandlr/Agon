Expressions and motion

Every face and every movement is a readout of simulation state. A viewer should be able to tell what an agent needs without opening the inspector. Faces are picked by AgonArt.expressionFor; movement by AgonArt.anim.

Faces

Twelve faces. The rules run top to bottom and the first match wins, so a dying agent never shows a happy face. Eyes, brows, mouth and one extra tell (sweat, tear, Z, tint) carry the meaning; no face relies on color alone.

Universal interaction layer

Everything physical is a thing with tags and numeric properties. A verb reads the properties of what it is used with and what it is done to, so combat, crafting, fire and eating run through one rule set. U means the thing can be used for the verb, O means the verb can be done to it, B means both. Combat damage in the simulation is computed by the hit verb here.

Decay: from fresh to skeleton

A body decays over {{decayTicks}} ticks (three days) after death. Decay is one number from 0 to 1: skin pales, bloats, greens and browns, flesh falls away in ragged patches, then only bones are left. In X-ray the organs rot first. The loop below plays the whole sequence; drag the slider to stop on any frame.

Animals

Same side-on view, ink outline and one shade step as the people. Thirty-five species: the original nine, then twenty-six more that only spawn in their native climates. Prey are rounded with the eye wide; predators are long and low with a yellow eye. Every animal also gets its own size (x0.85 to x1.2) and girth (lean to stout), so no two look the same. A yellow mark means alert, Z means resting after an attack, a bandage means hurt.
