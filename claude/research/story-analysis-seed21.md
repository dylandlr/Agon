# What the first continuous world's record says (seed 21, 8 founders, Y1 Spring to Y3 Spring)

2026-10-24. Source: `events.jsonl` (11,728 events), the terrain regenerated from map seed 21,
`lives.json`. The story is `docs/stories/story_caveman_seed21.txt`. Insights came from
aggregating the events; the tick-by-tick story was needed only to check single moments.

## The site decided most of the deaths

The band's camp cells, (14,11) and (4,26) and their neighbours, are forest beside marsh. That is
a good choice for warmth (forest halves the cold) and for water (the marsh is adjacent: 587 of
the drinks were taken at (4,26) and 556 at (14,11) without leaving camp). It is also where the
deaths came from:

- Marsh water is the dirty water. 12 of 19 infections name "the water it drank"; the rest spread
  by contact from those. Two deaths of disease (Dov, Gant).
- Crocodiles live in the marsh. All 6 crocodile attacks were on camp cells, (13,11), (12,11),
  (4,26), (3,25). Two deaths (Bren from 2% health, Cael carrying his child). The three wolf
  bites did no lasting harm.

The band never moved camp in two years (115 distinct cells used in all, the top eight all within
the two camps). Nothing in the record suggests they connected sickness or crocodiles with the
place. With 82 water cells on the map, there were other waters.

## The first winter: a cold snap, and who was alone

A cold snap came on Winter D4 (air -21 C on the tundra edge at (3,27), -9 to -15 at the camps).
In forest at 0 C air the thermal load is -0.9: damage even in forest unless resting (-0.75,
still damage) with at least two huddled neighbours (-0.6). So the snap was survivable only
together and at rest.

- Hael (male, 0.3 touches a day, the band's loner) spent his last two days on his bed at (4,26)
  drinking every half hour, alone, in near-zero air, and froze on D7 at 10:30. He drank 12 times a
  day over his life, twice anyone else, and 67 times in his last six days. Drinking is a standing
  act; he never rested and no one was beside him.
- Esk (female) sat at (12,11) through the snap: 46 drinks and 1 gathering in six days; starved
  on D8 with water at hand. Her brain had the drink habit and not the eating one (2.5 meals a day,
  the lowest).
- Ash (female) roamed 10 cells in her last six days, 44 drinks, 40 gatherings, 10 meals, a wolf
  bite; froze on D9.

Dov was downed in the same week and got up 164 hours later in spring. Fenn was "hardened" by it.

## Habits that cost them

- No day-night rhythm in foraging: 32% of gathering, drinking and eating happened in the eight
  night hours (a third of the day: the same rate as by day). Sleep peaks at dusk (17:00, 179
  fallings-asleep) and again from midnight to 05:00; they wake in the small hours and forage.
  In winter the night is when the open and even the forest kill.
- Overdrinking: Hael 12, Dov 9.9, Gant 7.2 drinks a day against 3-5 meals. The decision report's
  "17 of 17 drinks at full water" is this. It is not free: it is time standing, and for Esk it
  crowded out eating.
- Hive robbing never stopped paying its price: Cael was stung 8 times over two years, Esk and Fenn
  2 each. Honey was gathered 11 times.
- Fighting was constant and pointless: 32 blows, mostly Cael (15) and Ash (7), on each other,
  none lethal, none with a cause the record can name except two "striking back".

## Mating and the one conception

28 pairings. Bren was in 20 of them (Esk 8, Cael 7, Gant 3, Ash 2). The year-2 drop (22 to 6)
has a plain cause: two of the four females died in the first winter, and Bren, the band's main
male, was mauled by a crocodile, downed, sick and "haunted" through Y2 summer and killed in Y2
autumn. Cael conceived his child in Y2 summer and was killed in Y3 spring carrying it. Gant
(female) was never seen mating after Y1.

## What they did well

Camp in forest by water; bedding on leaves (722 beddings); sharing food left on the ground (Hael
to Dov, Dov to Hael, Bren to Gant); standing their ground against predators (5 times, 3 by Cael);
an aurochs tamed by what Hael left. A band of eight with no fire and no hunting survived two
winters and a drought. The cold snap and the marsh did the rest.

## Do we need tick by tick?

No, not for insights: every point above came from counting and cross-referencing the events
(positions against the terrain, acts per agent per day, causes). The tick-by-tick story is
for checking a single moment, and it earned its place once here: Hael's last 36 hours show the
exact failure (drink, drink, drink, bed, freeze) that the counts only suggest. Keep both: the
story for reading and for checking, the counts for claims.

## What this changes

1. Drinking at a full need has a real cost here (a cold night standing, a winter of not eating).
   The reward does not see it. Worth a small cost for an act that changes nothing, or a flatter
   need curve near full.
2. Night foraging is the behaviour to watch in the entropy-floor run: if the stall lifts, the
   night share should fall in winter.
3. Site choice (dirty water, crocodiles) is beyond what a brain with no memory of place-and-sickness
   can learn in two years; it is what a world model with a longer horizon, or culture across
   generations, is for.
