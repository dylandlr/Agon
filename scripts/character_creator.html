Agon character creator

Every person in Agon is drawn from this body model: segment lengths from shares of stature (Drillis & Contini, in Winter's tables) that follow growth with age, breadths from frame, body mass index and sex, and poses as joint angles. The map sprite, the inspector and this page draw from the same file, scripts/body_model.js.
